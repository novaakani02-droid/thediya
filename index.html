01 · THE GIRL BEHIND THE NAME

A rare kind of beautiful.

SELFLESS · GENUINE · KINDHEARTED

Some people have a way of making everyone around them feel seen, remembered and loved. Diya is one of those people.

A genuine heart, a beautiful spirit, and a kindness that speaks louder than words. From secondary school corridors to navigating miles apart, she remains the friend who shows up, who checks in when life gets noisy, and who holds onto her faith with inspiring devotion.

“To say you’re an amazing friend is an understatement; honestly, everyone deserves a Diya and we are so blessed we have ours.”

05 · THE FINAL TRIBUTE

A NEW CHAPTER BEGINS

“May 21 be as beautiful as everything you are becoming.”

Dear Diya, Twenty-one looks beautiful on you. May Allah bless your new chapter with ease, endless barakah and beautiful opportunities. May your hard work bear fruit, your silent prayers find answers and your heart always find reasons to be grateful. May you continue to grow, flourish and become everything you are meant to be. Never forget how deeply you are loved. Happy 21st birthday, Mardiya. Until the next reunion, the next laughter, the next gist and the next kenkey date. With all our love, always.

— Matilda, Phyllis, Franca & Vanessa ♡
